# Goals

PsiOps combat system philosophy:

* spatially abstract
* fiction-first
* tactically meaningful
* fast to adjudicate
* lethal
* no simulation overhead

The PsiOps combat system philosophy emphasizes:

- a spatially abstract approach,
- prioritizes fiction,
- remains tactically meaningful,
- ensures quick adjudication,
- maintains lethality,
- and avoids simulation overhead.

## 🎯 Core Takeaway  
A **zone system** treats the battlefield as a handful of meaningful areas—each with its own tactical properties—rather than a grid or a linear strip. Characters move between zones, not inches. Ranges become **relationships between zones**, not measurements. This preserves tactical clarity while keeping the game fast, gritty, and OSR‑lean.

## 🧭 What Zones *Are* (and Are Not)

Zones are **discrete chunks of space** that matter tactically. They are not distances; they are **areas of influence**.

A zone is defined by:
- **What it contains** (cover, vantage, hazards)
- **How it connects** to other zones
- **How hard it is to move into/out of**

Think of zones as the answer to:  

**"What parts of this scene matter tactically?"**  

not  

**"Where exactly is everyone standing?"**

Examples of zones in a modern firefight:

- **Streetfront** — cars, dumpsters, low cover  
- **Upper Floor** — elevation, windows, overwatch  
- **Back Alley** — concealment, flanking route  
- **Lobby** — choke point, hard cover  
- **Rooftop** — long sightlines, exposed  

Zones are **not**:

- 5‑ft squares  
- exact distances  
- linear range bands  
- simulationist geometry 

# Modes

## 🎮 Switching Between Narrative & Tactical Modes  

Your game wants to freely shift between:

- **Narrative mode** (loose, descriptive)  
- **Combat mode** (structured, tactical) 

Use this rule:

> **When precise spatial relationships matter, drop into zones.  
> When they stop mattering, collapse back to narrative.**

Example:
- Players approach a warehouse → narrative  
- Sniper fires from rooftop → switch to zones  
- Fight ends → collapse back to narrative  

Zones are a **temporary tactical overlay**, not a permanent map.
