# Game Modes Outline

Narrative vs Combat

The game is played in two distinct modes: *narrative* and *combat*. The modes differ significantly in pacing and in how they handle time and distance. They'll transition back and forth as mission events and narrative focus shift.

Most of the game unfolds in Operational Time.

Two modes:

- Operational

  - Distances
- Tactical

The flow of operational time is driven entirely by your actions and in-game events. Exact intervals and measurements aren’t tracked unless they’re important to the unfolding story. Distances and timeframes are flexible and arbitrary, dictated by the needs of the current situation. The GM describes time and distances in open-ended terms, adding precision only when needed, typically in response to your questions.



Distances in the narrative flow are entirely arbitrary and dictated by the needs of the current situation.

For example, if your unit advances through wooded terrain without any intervening events or challenges, the GM simply describes the maneuver, and you all move on to the next scene.

> We move out, well spaced, with Snakebite on point.
>
> The snow picks up as you move through the woods. You make it to the tree line without spotting anything and hunker down. What do you do?

If time were important to the maneuver — arriving at dawn — you simply arrive at dawn.
