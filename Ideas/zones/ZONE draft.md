## Operation Distances and Tactical Zones

Distances in the game are measured in two distinct scales: *operational distance* and *tactical zones*. These two scales flow into and out of each other as the game's pace and focus shift.

### Operational Distances

Distances in the narrative flow are entirely arbitrary and dictated by the needs of the current situation. The GM describes distances in open-ended terms, adding precision only in response to your questions.

Continuing the example from the previous section on maneuvering through a forest, if the GM presents a challenge in which a target bunker lies across a clear-cut field, the distance is described in general terms.

> It's a short distance to the bunker, but you'll be exposed crossing the field.
>
> Okay, first team dashes across while the second team covers.

There's no need to establish that the gap is 100 meters wide or that the operators could dash across at 3–5 meters per second.

### Tactical Zones

PsiOps does not track combat in meters, squares, or miniature-scale movement. Tactical combat is handled through **zones**: meaningful areas of the battlefield defined by position, exposure, cover, and tactical purpose.

A zone is not an exact measurement. It is a place that matters.

Examples:
- a rooftop firing position,
- a burning hallway,
- a street under sniper observation,
- a laboratory full of unstable psychic equipment.

The question is never:

> "Exactly where are you standing?"

The question is:

> "What tactical position are you holding?"
