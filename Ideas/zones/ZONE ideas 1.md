# Tactical Zone Operations
### PSIOPS Field Manual — Combat Positioning

---

**Forget the grid. Forget the ruler. Forget counting squares.**

If you've come up through tabletop combat with a battlemat and measuring tape, this will feel wrong at first. Lean into it. By the end of your first operation, you won't miss the ruler.

---

## Positions, Not Coordinates

A tactical zone isn't a map square. It's a *position* — a place that means something. The Loading Dock. The Overwatch Nest. The Burning Corridor. Each zone tells you what's around you, what you can do from here, and what happens to you if you linger.

The question isn't *where exactly am I?* It's *what kind of position am I in?*

Every zone has a tactical identity: cover, elevation, exposure, a choke, a hazard. If two areas are identical in every meaningful way — merge them. A zone without identity is just scenery.

---

## Zone Distances

Range is counted in zone hops — the number of zone boundaries between you and a target, following connections.

| Hops | Range |
|---|---|
| 0 | **Engaged** — same zone, sharing air |
| 1 | **Nearby** — across the room, over a barrier |
| 2 | **Far** — across a street, through a window |
| 3 | **Distant** — needs real marksmanship |
| 4+ | **Extreme** — sniper country |

Count the shortest path through connected zones — not a straight line on a map.

---

## Moving Between Zones

Crossing into an adjacent zone costs one action. You can still act afterward — fire, interact, go to ground.

Some crossings complicate things:

- **Difficult terrain** (rubble, standing water, thick smoke): costs 2 actions to push through.
- **Suppressed zone**: entering a zone under active fire requires a test — fail and you stop short or go to ground.
- **Free transitions**: slipping through an open interior door, moving within the same building floor. No action required; the GM narrates it.

---

## Zone Traits

Traits are attached to the zone — they apply to everyone inside it, and affect anyone shooting into or out of it.

| Trait | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Cover** | Hard barriers; ranged attacks in suffer penalties |
| **Elevated** | High ground; better sightlines, harder to flank |
| **Exposed** | No cover; you are the target |
| **Chokepoint** | One person through at a time; defender's ground |
| **Obscured** | Smoke, darkness, rain; hard to see, hard to hit |
| **Hazardous** | Fire, gas, collapse; being here costs you each round |

Traits can be created mid-fight. Throw smoke — you just made a zone Obscured. Flip a car — instant Cover. The battlefield is yours to shape if you move fast enough.

---

## The Operational Difference

Outside of structured combat, your GM describes distances in open terms. *The bunker is close — but you'll be exposed crossing the field.* No measurements. No numbers. Precision only when it matters.

When the shooting starts, zones kick in. Position becomes the whole game.

*Don't ask how far. Ask where you need to be.*
