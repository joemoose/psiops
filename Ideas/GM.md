# GM Rules

## Reaction Rolls

When you first contact a local national or a civilian contact whose disposition is unknown or uncertain, the GM can roll **2D6** and consult the table below to determine the contact's reaction.

| 2 | 3–5 | 6–8 | 9–11 | 12 |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Hostile | Wary | Curious | Friendly | Helpful |

## Morale 

Not all enemy forces fight to the bitter end. When morale collapses, they may retreat, surrender, or flee.

When enemies suffer their **first casualty** or lose **half** of their starting numbers, they make a *morale check*. A failed morale check causes them to break off the fight. Fanatical or mindless opponents don't make morale checks.

### Making a Morale Check

A hostile's *morale* is rated from **1** (an untrained, scared conscript) to **5** (a well-trained, elite professional).

The GM will tell you when your opponent must make a *morale check* and what their *morale rating* is. You then roll **1D6**. If the result exceeds their *morale rating*, the opposing force loses cohesion and either flees in disorder or withdraws in good order, depending on the unit's morale.

!!! note Notes

	- If a unit is led, morale checks use the **leader's** rating.
	- **Lone enemies** make a morale check when they first take damage and again when they lose half their hit points.
	- Morale checks don't apply to your unit. You decide when to withdraw.
