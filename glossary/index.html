    Glossary¶

    A reference of the game’s core terminology

    A¶

    Advancement¶

    Improving your character’s abilities after a mission by fulfilling your core drive’s advancement condition. See operator.

    Action¶

    Any meaningful task your operator attempts. If the outcome is risky or uncertain, you make an action roll to determine what happens.

    Action Roll¶

    The core mechanic of PSIOPS: roll 2d6 + applicable attribute to resolve a risky action. Results fall into four tiers: failure, partial success, success, critical success.

    Advantage¶

    Roll 3d6 instead of 2d6, then drop the lowest die. See disadvantage.

    Ambush¶

    A surprise attack that changes the first round of combat. - If you ambush: you act first with advantage; enemies take no turn. - If you are ambushed: you take no turn in round one.

    Applicable Attribute¶

    The attribute most relevant to how you describe your action. You choose which of the four attributes applies; the GM confirms. Some named rolls (initiative, wound, defense) specify a required attribute regardless of description.

    Armor Rating¶

    How much damage your protective gear reduces. Capped at 3 points.

    Attribute¶

    Numerical rating that defines your character’s innate capabilities and sets a modifier for related action rolls. Characters have four attributes: STRENGTH, REFLEXES, TACTICS, WILLPOWER.

    B¶

    Ballistic Armor¶

    The primary layer of your personal protective system. Reduces damage primarily from firearms. Some types of damage bypass ballistic armor entirely. Can be augmented with secondary armor.

    C¶

    Combat Mode¶

    Zoomed‑in, tactical play using rounds, zones, and structured actions.

    Complication¶

    A cost or trade-off that occurs on a partial success. You achieve your goal, but something goes wrong — you lose equipment, create a new threat, or face a difficult choice.

    Concentration¶

    Sustaining an active neural weapon effect. Each round you maintain concentration, you must spend your action to do so. Taking damage breaks concentration and ends the effect.

    Consequence¶

    A negative outcome that occurs on a failed action roll.

    Cover¶

    Terrain or objects that make you harder to hit. Attacking from cover typically grants advantage on your defense rolls.

    Core Drive¶

    Your operator’s personal motivation and advancement trigger. You advance by fulfilling its condition during a mission.

    Critical Success¶

    A roll of 13+ on an action roll. You achieve your goal and more.

    D¶

    d3¶

    Roll one six-sided die, divide the result by two, and round up.

    d6¶

    Roll one six-sided die.

    2d6¶

    Roll two six-sided dice and add the results.

    d66¶

    Roll two six-sided dice, but instead of adding the results, read them in order to form a 2-digit number. For example, a 2 followed by a 4 is 24. A 1 followed by another 1 is 11.

    Damage Modifier¶

    A flat bonus added to damage when an attack succeeds. Determined by weapon type.

    Damage Reduction¶

    The amount of damage reduced by your personal protective system and hard cover.

    Defense Roll¶

    When an enemy attacks you, you roll 2d6 + applicable attribute to avoid or reduce damage.

    Disadvantage¶

    Roll 3d6 instead of 2d6, then drop the highest die. See advantage.

    Distance¶

    Range is measured in hops between zones: Close (0), Nearby (1), Far (2), Distant (3), Extreme (4+).

    F¶

    Failure¶

    A roll of 6 or lower on an action roll. You don’t achieve your goal and suffer an additional consequence.

    Fatigue¶

    A physical/mental toll that occupies inventory slots. If fatigue fills all 12 slots, you die.

    Flashback¶

    A momentum spend (6 points) that lets you retroactively establish a preparation or discovery made before or during the mission. The GM must agree the flashback is plausible.

    G¶

    Game Master (GM)¶

    The player who describes the world, portrays enemies and NPCs, adjudicates rules, and presents the mission. The GM doesn’t play an operator.

    H¶

    Hard Cover¶

    A type of cover that provides damage reduction.

    Hit Points (HP)¶

    Your physical health. At 0 or below, you make a wound roll.

    Hops¶

    See Distance.

    I¶

    Initiative Roll¶

    A one‑time roll at the start of combat to determine whether you act in round one.

    Inventory Slots¶

    The 12 slots that represent your carrying capacity. Each item typically occupies 1 or 2 slots. Fatigue occupies slots, crowding out gear.

    L¶

    Locked-In¶

    A positive stress-threshold effect. When you roll 13+ on a stress roll, you clear all stress and gain a combat buff determined by a 1d6 roll.

    M¶

    Maximum HP¶

    The total HP your player character has. Can be reduced by wounds.

    Mission Clock¶

    A segmented progress tracker used for complex tasks or impending threats.

    Mob¶

    A large group of weak enemies treated as a single opponent with scaling armor and attacks.

    Momentum¶

    A tactical resource earned on successful action rolls and spent to boost actions, avoid consequences, or take extra actions.

    N¶

    Neural Weapon¶

    Your paracognitive powers activated with a WILLPOWER roll.

    O¶

    Operational Mode¶

    Zoomed‑out play focused on movement, planning, infiltration, and problem‑solving.

    Operator¶

    Your player character: a classified, off-ledger military operative with anomalous cognitive abilities.

    P¶

    Panicked¶

    A negative stress-threshold effect. When you roll 6 or lower on a stress roll, you panic and suffer a debilitating condition determined by a 1d6 roll.

    Partial Success¶

    A roll of 7–9: you succeed, but with a complication.

    Personal Protective System¶

    Your armor: ballistic armor and secondary armor. Reduces damage.

    R¶

    Rally Point¶

    A once‑per‑mission safe location where you reset momentum, clear stress effects, and choose one recovery benefit.

    Range¶

    See Distance.

    REFLEXES¶

    An attribute that measures your character’s innate agility and speed.

    Round (Combat Round)¶

    A ~6‑second slice of combat in which you may move once and take 1 action.

    S¶

    Secondary Armor¶

    Part of the personal protective system consisting of helmets and riot shields that provide a damage reduction bonus to ballistic armor.

    Specialization (Advanced Specialization)¶

    A binary skill that grants +1 to relevant action rolls.

    Squad¶

    The group of operators — the player characters — working together on a mission.

    STRENGTH¶

    An attribute that measures your character’s brawn and endurance.

    Stress¶

    A meter tracking psychological strain. At 6, you make a stress roll and may become panicked or locked-in.

    Stress Roll¶

    A roll made when your stress reaches 6: roll 2d6 + WILLPOWER. Results: 2–6 you panic; 7–9 you hold together; 10–12 you hold together and remove 1 stress; 13+ you lock in.

    Success¶

    A roll of 10–12 on an action roll. You achieve your goal cleanly, with no complication.

    Suppressing Fire¶

    An action that pins all targets in a zone and grants free attacks if they expose themselves.

    T¶

    Tactical Supplies¶

    Abstract gear slots you can convert into specific equipment during the mission.

    TACTICS¶

    An attribute that measures your character’s logical thinking and training.

    Total Cover¶

    A type of cover that prevents you from being targeted.

    W¶

    WILLPOWER¶

    An attribute that measures your character’s presence and resolve.

    Wound Roll¶

    A roll made when your HP hits 0 or below to determine whether you die, are incapacitated, or stay on your feet.

    Wounds¶

    Permanent injuries that reduce your maximum HP until treated at base.

    Z¶

    Zone¶

    A discrete tactical area used in combat instead of a grid. Movement is zone‑to‑zone, not by feet or squares.
