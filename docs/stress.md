# Stress and Fatigue

## Operational Preface

During a mission, you will accumulate *stress* — the mental and physical wear from constant danger, sustained vigilance, and traumatic exposure. On 

## Gaining Stress

A *stress rating* tracks your overall stress, starting at **0** at the start of your mission. As the mission progresses, events and circumstances will add to your rating.

Common sources of stress include:

- Encountering anomalous entities, locations, or events
- Failing an *action roll*
- Going over 24 hours without rest, food, or water
- Knocked unconscious
- Riding in a vehicle hit by a heavy weapon
- Running out of ammunition
- Sustaining an *injury*
- Taking damage
- Thrown into darkness in hostile territory
- Witnessing a team member or civilian die

Keep track of the stress points you accumulate as events unfold during the mission. Your character sheet has a place to record your current stress. Typically, each source adds **1** stress point. The GM will inform you when mission events or your actions increase your stress. 

## Reaching Maximum Stress

Left unchecked, stress can degrade your performance and increase the risk of critical failures.

Your stress rating caps at **6**. Whenever a stress point raises your rating to **6**, your combat readiness is at risk. Make a **stress check** (*action roll*): roll **2D6 + WILLPOWER** and apply the result.

Any stress points beyond **6** are ignored. However, after resolving a *stress check*, you may still be at **6** points. If so, no further check is required. Only the next stress point that pushes a **6** rating triggers a new check.

<div class="roll-table" markdown="1">

| Roll | Result |
|:----:|:-------|
| **6–** | You **panic**. Roll **1D6** and see the following [Panicking table](panicking) for the effect. |
| **7–9** | You **keep it together** for now. |
| **10–12** | You **keep it together** and remove **1** stress point. |
| **13+** | You are completely **locked into the flow** of combat. Remove **all** stress, then roll **1D6** and see the following [Locked-In table](locked-in) for the effect. |

</div>

All *Panicking* and *Locked-In* effects last until the **end** of the mission or until you **rest** at a *rendezvous point*. See [Rest and Recovery](combat.md#rest-and-recovery).

!!! example

	You take damage from a gunshot (stress: 5), then watch your contact be executed (stress: 6). You make a stress check: rolls a 4 and 3, adds your WILLPOWER of +2 for a total of 9. You keep it together — for now.

## Reducing Stress

You can reduce your stress during a mission by following these steps. Each option removes **1** stress point. 

- Spend your *momentum*. See [Momentum](playing.md#momentum).
- Rest at a *rendezvous point*. See [Rest and Recovery](combat.md#rest-and-recovery).
- Overcome a *notable opponent* or *major obstacle*.
- **Notable opponent**: A named or significant adversary whose defeat materially advances the mission: the primary target, a commanding officer, or a recurring nemesis.
	- **Major obstacle**: A mission-critical barrier that, if unaddressed, prevents task completion or forces an abort: a security lockdown, a timed demolition, a failed extraction point, or a severe environmental hazard.

### Panicking

Whenever you *panic*, roll **1D6** and apply the result. If you panic multiple times, the effects are cumulative. 

| D6 | Condition | Effect |
| :-: | :-: | - |
| **1** | Afraid | *Disadvantage* on all your *action rolls* in combat. |
| **2** | Hopeless | You fail all your *initiative rolls*. |
| **3** | Insulting | Allies gain **1** stress whenever you see them fail an *action roll*. |
| **4** | Selfish | *Disadvantage* on all your *action rolls* that could benefit a team member. |
| **5** | Shutdown | You lose all your *momentum* and no longer gain any. |
| **6** | Weak | You have **-1 ARMOR**. |

### Locked-In

Whenever you’re locked in, roll **1D6** and apply the result. If you lock in multiple times, the effects are cumulative.

| D6 | Condition | Effect |
| :-: | :-: | - |
| **1** | Cunning | *Advantage* on all +TACTICS rolls. |
| **2** | Focused | *Advantage* on all +WILLPOWER rolls. |
| **3** | Hardened | You gain +1 ARMOR. |
| **4** | Jacked | *Advantage* on all +STRENGTH rolls. |
| **5** | Sharp | *Advantage* on all +REFLEXES rolls. |
| **6** | Steady | Whenever you succeed on an *action roll*, team members within sight of you remove **1** stress. |
