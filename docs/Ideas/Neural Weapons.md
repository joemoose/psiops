# Neural Weapons

Lots of cool terms.

Official:

Psi-Class Ordinance
Anomalous Capabilities
Psi Assets
Unit slang:

Tricks
Brainburn
The Other Kit
Missions specifically using the powers:

Directives: Enhanced Cognition Package (ECP)
Slang: Ghostworks
Civilians with the powers:

Unregistered Capabilities



Here are a few directions, each with a different tonal register:

---

**Classified/bureaucratic dread** *(reads like a redacted field manual)*

> Neural Weapons are not a metaphor. They are not folklore. They are Psi-Class Ordnance — documented, field-tested, and detected in military personnel who demonstrate Anomalous Capabilities under Program evaluation. You carry them the way other operators carry explosives: with training, with discipline, and with the understanding that misuse is a life-ending one.

---

**Punchy/operational** *(briefing room, no-nonsense)*

> Every operator gets a kit. Yours has an extra compartment. The Other Kit — what the brass calls your Anomalous Capabilities — doesn't appear on any manifest, doesn't show up on any scan, and cannot be surrendered at the border. It is a weapon. Train it accordingly.

---

**Cold and clinical with an edge** *(think DARPA memo meets field horror)*

> The Program does not use the word *psychic*. What you have are Neural Weapons — Psi-Class Ordnance, cataloged, classified, and assigned a threat rating by the same office that handles chemical munitions. This section contains your complete capabilities inventory. Study it. The opposition already has theirs.

---

**Terse/evocative** *(shortest, most stylized)*

> They called it Brainburn before anyone called it anything official. The Program gave it a designation. The designation gave it a budget. The budget found it in you. What follows is a complete inventory of known capabilities.

---

My instinct is that the **bureaucratic dread** version has the best legs for a manual — it establishes tone, uses your official terminology naturally, and that last sentence lands with real weight. But the **terse/evocative** one would be killer as a *sidebar* or pull quote alongside the D66 table.
