Neural Weapons alternate names

Four editorial flags before the table:

**Duplicate brainstorm entries** — "Overclock" appears in both Attribute Boost and Augmented Speed; "Deep Cover" appears in both Hibernation and Thousand Faces. Both pairs will need to be resolved before committing to names.

**"Mindlink"** (Telepathy column 2) — this one has been kicking around TTRPGs as long as Telepathy itself. Contradicts the stated goal.

**"Washout"** (Recover column 2) — in military context, to wash out means to fail selection (washed out of SEAL training). Conflicts with a power that removes negative effects.
