# Glossary
*A reference of the game's core terminology*

---

## A

#### Advancement

Improving your character's abilities after a mission by fulfilling your **[core drive's](#core-drive)** advancement condition. See **[operator](#operator)**.

#### Action

Any meaningful task your **[operator](#operator)** attempts. If the outcome is risky or uncertain, you make an **[action roll](#action-roll)** to determine what happens.

#### Action Roll

The core mechanic of PSIOPS: roll **2d6 + [applicable attribute](#applicable-attribute)** to resolve a risky action.
Results fall into four tiers: **[failure](#failure)**, **[partial success](#partial-success)**, **[success](#success)**, **[critical success](#critical-success)**.

#### Advantage

Roll **3d6** instead of 2d6, then drop the *lowest* die. See **[disadvantage](#disadvantage)**.

#### Ambush

A surprise attack that changes the first round of combat.
- If *you* ambush: you act first with **[advantage](#advantage)**; enemies take no turn.
- If *you are ambushed*: you take no turn in **[round](#round-combat-round)** one.

#### Applicable Attribute

The **[attribute](#attribute)** most relevant to how you describe your action. You choose which of the four attributes applies; the **[GM](#game-master-gm)** confirms. Some named rolls (**[initiative](#initiative-roll)**, **[wound](#wound-roll)**, **[defense](#defense-roll)**) specify a required attribute regardless of description.

#### Armor Rating

How much damage your protective gear reduces. Capped at **3** points.

#### Attribute

Numerical rating that defines your character's innate capabilities and sets a modifier for related action rolls. Characters have four attributes: **[STRENGTH](#strength)**, **[REFLEXES](#reflexes)**, **[TACTICS](#tactics)**, **[WILLPOWER](#willpower)**.

---

## B

#### Ballistic Armor

The primary layer of your **[personal protective system](#personal-protective-system)**. Reduces damage primarily from firearms. Some types of damage bypass ballistic armor entirely. Can be augmented with **[secondary armor](#secondary-armor)**.

---

## C

#### Combat Mode

Zoomed‑in, tactical play using **[rounds](#round-combat-round)**, **[zones](#zone)**, and structured actions.

#### Complication

A cost or trade-off that occurs on a **[partial success](#partial-success)**. You achieve your goal, but something goes wrong — you lose equipment, create a new threat, or face a difficult choice.

#### Concentration

Sustaining an active **[neural weapon](#neural-weapon)** effect. Each round you maintain concentration, you must spend your **[action](#action)** to do so. Taking damage breaks concentration and ends the effect.

#### Consequence

A negative outcome that occurs on a failed **[action roll](#action-roll)**.

#### Cover

Terrain or objects that make you harder to hit. Attacking from cover typically grants **[advantage](#advantage)** on your **[defense rolls](#defense-roll)**.

#### Core Drive

Your **[operator's](#operator)** personal motivation and **[advancement](#advancement)** trigger. You advance by fulfilling its condition during a mission.

#### Critical Success

A roll of **13+** on an **[action roll](#action-roll)**. You achieve your goal *and more*.

---

## D

#### d3

Roll one six-sided die, divide the result by two, and round up.

#### d6

Roll one six-sided die.

#### 2d6

Roll two six-sided dice and add the results.

#### d66

Roll two six-sided dice, but instead of adding the results, read them in order to form a 2-digit number. For example, a **2** followed by a **4** is **24**. A **1** followed by another **1** is **11**.

#### Damage Modifier

A flat bonus added to damage when an attack succeeds. Determined by weapon type.

#### Damage Reduction

The amount of damage reduced by your **[personal protective system](#personal-protective-system)** and **[hard cover](#hard-cover)**.

#### Defense Roll

When an enemy attacks you, *you* roll **2d6 + [applicable attribute](#applicable-attribute)** to avoid or reduce damage.

#### Disadvantage

Roll **3d6** instead of 2d6, then drop the *highest* die. See **[advantage](#advantage)**.

#### Distance

Range is measured in **[hops](#hops)** between **[zones](#zone)**: Close (0), Nearby (1), Far (2), Distant (3), Extreme (4+).

---

## F

#### Failure

A roll of **6 or lower** on an **[action roll](#action-roll)**. You don't achieve your goal and suffer an additional **[consequence](#consequence)**.

#### Fatigue

A physical/mental toll that occupies **[inventory slots](#inventory-slots)**. If fatigue fills all 12 slots, you die.

#### Flashback

A **[momentum](#momentum)** spend (6 points) that lets you retroactively establish a preparation or discovery made before or during the mission. The **[GM](#game-master-gm)** must agree the flashback is plausible.

---

## G

#### Game Master (GM)

The player who describes the world, portrays enemies and NPCs, adjudicates rules, and presents the mission. The GM doesn't play an **[operator](#operator)**.

---

## H

#### Hard Cover

A type of **[cover](#cover)** that provides **[damage reduction](#damage-reduction)**.

#### Hit Points (HP)

Your physical health. At **0 or below**, you make a **[wound roll](#wound-roll)**.

#### Hops

See **[Distance](#distance)**.

---

## I

#### Initiative Roll

A one‑time roll at the start of combat to determine whether you act in **[round](#round-combat-round)** one.

#### Inventory Slots

The 12 slots that represent your carrying capacity. Each item typically occupies 1 or 2 slots. **[Fatigue](#fatigue)** occupies slots, crowding out gear.

---

## L

#### Locked-In

A positive stress-threshold effect. When you roll **13+** on a **[stress roll](#stress-roll)**, you clear all stress and gain a combat buff determined by a **1d6** roll.

---

## M

#### Maximum HP

The total **[HP](#hit-points-hp)** your player character has. Can be reduced by **[wounds](#wounds)**.

#### Mission Clock

A segmented progress tracker used for complex tasks or impending threats.

#### Mob

A large group of weak enemies treated as a single opponent with scaling **[armor](#armor-rating)** and attacks.

#### Momentum

A tactical resource earned on successful **[action rolls](#action-roll)** and spent to boost actions, avoid **[consequences](#consequence)**, or take extra actions.

---

## N

#### Neural Weapon

Your paracognitive powers activated with a **[WILLPOWER](#willpower)** roll.

---

## O

#### Operational Mode

Zoomed‑out play focused on movement, planning, infiltration, and problem‑solving.

#### Operator

Your player character: a classified, off-ledger military operative with anomalous cognitive abilities.

---

## P

#### Panicked

A negative stress-threshold effect. When you roll **6 or lower** on a **[stress roll](#stress-roll)**, you panic and suffer a debilitating condition determined by a **1d6** roll.

#### Partial Success

A roll of **7–9**: you succeed, but with a **[complication](#complication)**.

#### Personal Protective System

Your armor: **[ballistic armor](#ballistic-armor)** and **[secondary armor](#secondary-armor)**. Reduces damage.

---

## R

#### Rally Point

A once‑per‑mission safe location where you reset **[momentum](#momentum)**, clear **[stress](#stress)** effects, and choose one recovery benefit.

#### Range

See **[Distance](#distance)**.

#### REFLEXES

An **[attribute](#attribute)** that measures your character's innate agility and speed.

#### Round (Combat Round)

A ~6‑second slice of combat in which you may **move once** and take **1 [action](#action)**.

---

## S

#### Secondary Armor

Part of the **[personal protective system](#personal-protective-system)** consisting of helmets and riot shields that provide a **[damage reduction](#damage-reduction)** bonus to **[ballistic armor](#ballistic-armor)**.

#### Specialization (Advanced Specialization)

A binary skill that grants **+1** to relevant **[action rolls](#action-roll)**.

#### Squad

The group of **[operators](#operator)** — the player characters — working together on a mission.

#### STRENGTH

An **[attribute](#attribute)** that measures your character's brawn and endurance.

#### Stress

A meter tracking psychological strain. At **6**, you make a **[stress roll](#stress-roll)** and may become **[panicked](#panicked)** or **[locked-in](#locked-in)**.

#### Stress Roll

A roll made when your stress reaches **6**: roll **2d6 + [WILLPOWER](#willpower)**. Results: **2–6** you [panic](#panicked); **7–9** you hold together; **10–12** you hold together and remove 1 stress; **13+** you [lock in](#locked-in).

#### Success

A roll of **10–12** on an **[action roll](#action-roll)**. You achieve your goal cleanly, with no complication.

#### Suppressing Fire

An **[action](#action)** that pins all targets in a **[zone](#zone)** and grants free attacks if they expose themselves.

---

## T

#### Tactical Supplies

Abstract gear slots you can convert into specific equipment during the mission.

#### TACTICS

An **[attribute](#attribute)** that measures your character's logical thinking and training.

#### Total Cover

A type of **[cover](#cover)** that prevents you from being targeted.

---

## W

#### WILLPOWER

An **[attribute](#attribute)** that measures your character's presence and resolve.

#### Wound Roll

A roll made when your HP hits **0 or below** to determine whether you die, are incapacitated, or stay on your feet.

#### Wounds

Permanent injuries that reduce your **[maximum HP](#maximum-hp)** until treated at base.

---

## Z

#### Zone

A discrete tactical area used in combat instead of a grid. Movement is zone‑to‑zone, not by feet or squares.
