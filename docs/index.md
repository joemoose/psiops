# Operational Preface

PSIOPS is a squad-based RPG set at the hidden edge of the Cold War. You and your unit are special forces operators running covert operations against threats that don't appear in any field manuals.

The system is built around a single mechanic: roll two six-sided dice, add an *attribute score*, and see how well you do. That’s it. The four attributes cover everything from your strength to your resolve. A *momentum* system rewards courageous action. A *stress* system tracks the things you might see and do that leave a mark. 

If this is your first role-playing game, you'll be up and running in minutes. If you're a veteran, the system will feel familiar from the start.

## Before We Begin

This game can explore dark themes of violence, psychological stress, and the paranormal. Before your first session, the GM and players should agree on which topics or themes should and *shouldn’t* be included in the game.

Anyone at the table can say the following at any time:

- **Pause** to take a break in the current scene.
- **Rewind** to revisit a prior scene.
- **Stop** to stop and completely remove the current scene.

If someone calls a Stop, don’t debate it. Just move on.

## Golden Rules

1. **People matter more than games**.
2. **Fun matters more than rules**.
3. **Your choices matter**.

Ask questions. Take chances. The game rewards risk.

# Game Overview

The core of PSIOPS is a conversation between you and the game master (GM). The GM describes your current mission situation. You and your fellow players then ask questions and tell the GM what your characters are doing. The GM explains the results of your actions, and you react again, describing more actions, and so on.

If the actions you describe are risky and the outcome isn't immediately clear, you'll roll the dice to determine the result.

The GM answers your questions and always respects your freedom of choice. The GM presents challenges, obstacles, and situations for you to solve and overcome during your mission. Still, the game is more than just a story narrated by the GM — it's an interactive narrative built on your decisions. Consider it as both creating and acting in an action film at the same time. 

## How to Use this Manual

In the next chapter, you'll create your character. You'll encounter game terms such as *Momentum*, *Tactical Supplies*, *Advantage*, and *Action Checks*. Don't worry about them yet. The game's underlying mechanics and rules are covered in later chapters.

Use your character sheet as a focal point while reading the manual. A [rules summary](appendix.md#rules-summary) is also included at the back of the manual. You may find it a useful reference during your sessions.
